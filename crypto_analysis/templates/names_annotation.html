Instructions (read before starting)

Each item is a topic of pump.fun tokens, shown by its most distinctive terms (c-TF-IDF) and 12 samples that cover the whole topic (image, and the token name, symbol and full description). An LLM (Qwen3.5-9B) looked at the same samples and wrote a name and a one-sentence summary for it. These names label the topics and most narratives in the paper.

  1. Does the name describe the topic? Yes: it fits what most samples share. Partly: right subject but too narrow, too broad or missing a main part. No: wrong or misleading. If not yes, write a better name (at most 6 words).
  2. Is the topic coherent? Yes when most samples share a subject; No when they look like a mix of unrelated tokens (then judge the name against the largest group of samples).
  • Annotate alone: do not discuss items with the other annotator until both of you have exported.
  • Answers are saved in this browser as you go. When finished, press Export CSV and send the file.
  • Keys: 1 yes, 2 partly, 3 no (name); ←/→ previous/next.
  • Some images may be withheld because an automatic check flagged them as possible sexual content involving minors; judge those samples from their text.

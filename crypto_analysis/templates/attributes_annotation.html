Instructions and definitions (read before starting)

Each item is a topic of pump.fun tokens: its name (written by an LLM), its most distinctive terms (c-TF-IDF) and 12 samples that cover the whole topic, each with its image, the token name, symbol and full description, and the share of the topic its region holds. Label every sample: tick each attribute it shows, or None; tick Other and name it if the sample shows harmful or sensitive content outside these attributes. A topic’s share of an attribute is computed later from the samples and their region shares.

  • Annotate alone: do not discuss items with the other annotator until both of you have exported.
  • Answers are saved in this browser as you go. When finished, press Export CSV and send the file.
  • Some images are withheld because an automatic check flagged them as possible sexual content involving minors; label those samples from their text.

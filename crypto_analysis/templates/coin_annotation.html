Instructions and definitions

Content warning: the sample contains racist, antisemitic, sexual and violent content.

Each card is a pump.fun coin: its image, name, symbol and description. For each coin, mark the narratives it belongs to and the attributes that are present. A coin can have several narratives or none, and several attributes. Every coin is a memecoin, so "memecoin", "token" or "Solana" is never a narrative or an attribute. Annotate independently, without discussing the coins with the other annotators.

Mark an attribute only when its definition is met; tone, intent and whether it is a joke do not matter. An object associated with an attribute (a weapon, a pill, a flag) is not enough by itself: what the coin shows or says must meet the definition. Do not guess what an unfamiliar symbol means. The definitions below are quoted from the cited papers and are the same ones given to the model.

Coins the model marked as sexual content involving minors are shown without their image. If you see such content in another coin, mark the attribute: its image is blurred at once. Do not save or share images.

Answers are saved in this browser. Click "Next" (top bar or end of the card, or press →) to record a coin as annotated (even with nothing marked), export the CSV when you finish (and now and then) and send the file. "Import CSV" resumes work exported earlier.
